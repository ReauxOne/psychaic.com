About

Psyche + archaic

Psychaic is my art practice. I studied interior design, spent many years at the potter's wheel, and grew up loving the future as magazines of the 1970s and 80s imagined it: polished, hopeful, a little strange, and full of questions.

Everything here is made from that feeling. Porcelain and platinum, chrome and sky, oxblood horizons. Old shapes meet new tomorrows.
